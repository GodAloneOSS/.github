# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

GodAloneOSS exists to benefit people, for the sake of Allah. That purpose only works if everyone who participates, no matter their country, language, background, or technical experience, is treated with respect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give constructive feedback, focused on the work rather than the person.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assuming bad intent.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Use inclusive, considerate language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language directed at anyone's background, language, nationality, or ability.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies within all GodAloneOSS repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainers by opening a private security advisory or contacting an organization owner directly. Reports will be handled with discretion and respect for the people involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with it, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is a simplified, project-specific document inspired by common open-source community guidelines such as the Contributor Covenant.
